# docs/archive

Superseded material kept for the auditable record. Current, authoritative documents are in
`docs/design-log/` (see its [`README.md`](../design-log/README.md) for reading order) and the
pre-registration is `docs/prereg.jsonl`.

## Removed from the published tree (2026-11-06, D1 public-release cleanup)

The following superseded directories were removed from the public release. They were internal
build-process scaffolding (early build order, task handoffs, research-grounding notes, one-off
experiment and ablation outputs), not part of the published research record, and several carried
stale internal references. They remain in the project's full history on the development branch.

- `docs/build-bundle/` — the early master build order, promotion artifacts, and morning reports.
- `docs/tasks/` — superseded task briefs and handoffs.
- `docs/research/` — early research-grounding notes.
- `docs/experiments/` — one-off experiment outputs.
- `docs/ablation_trials/` — ablation-ladder trial logs.
- `docs/arms_race_preserved/` — preserved early arms-race logs.

Also removed: `attic/` (the explicit discard pile).

What stays in `docs/archive/` is the material still referenced as context — including
`PHASE2_ENTERPRISE_ARMS_RACE.md` and the pinned pre-registration manifests
(`COEVO_PREREGISTRATION.md`, `EXPERIMENT_DESIGNS.md`, `PREREG_ENTERPRISE_ADAPTATION.md`). The
committed run archives under `docs/runs/` are unaffected.
